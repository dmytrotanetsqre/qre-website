Social proof (2026-10-04): Option 1's second section (title + two metrics, endless client-logo strip) in Option 2 type and colours, between the hero and services; the metric notes are Inter 14 normal case, not Option 1's mono uppercase. - **Option 3** — `option-3/` (`index.html`, `style.css`, `site.js`, `cta-q.js`, `script.js`, `crystal.js`, `crystal-lab.html`, `vendor/three.module.js`, `poster.jpg`; shares `../assets/`). **Dark source:** `_snapshots/option-1-dark-2026-10-22/` (not project-root Option 1). Full page dark: film hero, social proof, then every remaining snapshot section in order — Services as a scrolling list beside one sticky morphing crystal (2026-10-23, below), recent work with the WebGL hover lens, contact CTA as an open on-page form plus consultation drawer from the header, footer and logo strip. **Type (2026-10-22):** all UI text in Inter (`../assets/fonts/inter-latin-opsz-normal.woff2`) — titles Inter Display (`opsz` 32, tracking about −0.04em to −0.045em), body Inter (`opsz` 14, tracking about −0.01em), subheads about −0.02em; normal sentence case everywhere (no `text-transform: uppercase`). Geist Mono only inside the services crystal ASCII canvas. Pill buttons (999px radius): primary filled, outline filled on hover (Option 2 pattern); simple nav hover, no letter-roll. Nav and hero CTAs anchor `#services`, `#work`, `#contact`. **Hero intro (2026-10-24):** the eye video (`../assets/QRE-marketing-banner-video.mp4`) plays muted on loop, seen only through the QRE logo. Paths from `../assets/icons/qre-logo.svg` (viewBox 0 0 106 32) are an inline SVG clipPath (`#qre-logo-clip`, objectBoundingBox; the Q uses clip-rule evenodd). `poster.jpg` fills the mask until the video can play. On load the header is hidden and the page is locked (`html.is-intro`: overflow hidden, wheel/touch/keys blocked, `scrollbar-gutter: stable`). The sequence starts once the film has a frame (`requestVideoFrameCallback` / `canplay`, 4 s fallback) and plays every load. The logo sits at `mask.width` 0.65 of the stage (0.85 at ≤768) with the R's crossbar centre on the stage centre, fades and scales in over `mask.enter` 800 ms from `mask.enterFrom` 0.96, holds `mask.hold` 600 ms, then zooms `mask.zoomMs` 1600 ms (ease-in-out quartic) about `mask.zoom` (51.2259, 18.2222), the centre of the R's crossbar (`mask.stroke` x 37.6987–64.7531, y 15.5556–20.8889), until that bar covers the stage. The film scales `mask.videoFrom` 1.15 → `mask.videoTo` 1 over the zoom. A ±`mask.breath` 0.01 scale breath (`mask.breathPeriod` 6800) runs during the intro; the cover scale is divided by `1 - breath` so the low point still fills the frame. The copy then fades and rises (`mask.copy` 800 ms, `mask.copyShift` 16 px, outCubic) with the header (Get in touch outline pill opens the consultation drawer; at ≤960 it sits beside the menu toggle at 36 px). Click, tap, or any key skips to the end. After the intro the hero is a normal 100svh section (`.hero.is-ready`, no sticky pin, no `--hero-run`). Geometry is measured from the stage on resize; the logo stays 106:32. The film pauses when the hero leaves the view. `prefers-reduced-motion` (`html.is-reduced`): no intro, no lock, no zoom, full-bleed video and copy; `poster.jpg` if the video cannot play. No film grain, no 0/1 field, no gem preloader. **Services (2026-10-23):** desktop (≥1024) is a two-column sticky layout. The left column is the six services, each about one viewport tall, keeping its title, lede, bullet list and Learn more link. The right column (~46%) is one `position: sticky` panel, vertically centred under the header, holding one ASCII crystal — the contact gem's renderer (`asciiArt` in `script.js`) sampling a looping video. Below 1024 there is no split: the crystal is a 40svh bar stuck under the header and the list scrolls beneath it. The only service→video map is `SERVICE_VIDEOS` in `site.js`: AI Agent → `ai-agents.mp4` (the same crystal as the hero gem), Custom AI Models → `custom-ai-models.mp4`, Mobile & Desktop Apps → `mobile-desktop-apps.mp4`, Websites → `websites.mp4`, Business Automation → `business-automation.mp4`, Analytics → `analytics.mp4`, all in `assets/services/video/` (640×484). The service crossing the viewport midline is active (full strength); the others sit at 35%. A change keeps the current video playing and dissolves cell-by-cell into the next (~700 ms, ease-in-out, seeded clustered thresholds, a soft band and a brightness dip to about 60%); a fast scroll retargets from the current blend instead of queueing. The counter and the name change at the middle of the dissolve. The first video loads when the section nears the viewport; only the neighbours of the active service are preloaded, and only the active video plays at rest. The crystal pauses off-screen. `prefers-reduced-motion`: videos hold one frame and the canvas crossfades in 200 ms. **Crystal (`crystal.js`):** kept for `crystal-lab.html`. Services no longer mounts it. The visible canvas is a fixed Geist Mono 0/1 grid at the hero ASCII cell (14px, letter-spacing 0.5, 12px below 1024). Cells never move, rotate, or scale; each flips 0↔1 on its own slow clock. Three.js draws the shape as a solid mesh — key light plus rim, faces near brightness 0.62, rims toward white — into a small offscreen buffer. Each frame samples that buffer one pixel per cell (`getImageData`, the ASCII renderer's path) and uses the brightness as the cell's alpha, so cells outside the shape stay off and rotation reads as light moving across the still grid. The six shapes are meshes normalised to the same size. A service change crossfades the two meshes over 0.9s (in-out cubic, a slight scale); a newer target replaces the in-flight one from the current weights, so fast scrolling never queues. The mesh yaws once every 18s. `createCrystal`, `crystal-lab.html`. Three.js r170 lives in `vendor/three.module.js`. **Contact (2026-10-24):** desktop (≥1024) two columns — left ~45% heading centred over the logo Q (kicker “Ready to scale with AI?”, title sharing `.proof__title` / `.services__title` type, lead; a feathered wash behind the words only). `.cta__art` bleeds to the viewport left; `cta-q.js` draws the logo Q as a hidden-line slab in local Three.js — ExtrudeGeometry, no bevel, MeshBasicMaterial in `--surface` plus EdgesGeometry lines in `--paper` at ~0.9; camera isometric from above `(d, d×0.6, d)`, letter upright, yaw 24s linear, ±4° fine-pointer parallax; lazy-init within one viewport, paused off-screen, still ~30° yaw for reduced motion; canvas `pointer-events: none`. Right ~55% is only the consultation form, top aligned with the title (same fields, `.field` / `.consent` / pill styles, validation, and thank-you state as the drawer via `wireConsultationForm` in `site.js`); `<1024` the heading sits over the ~45svh Q, same bleed, form below. Section ≥100svh, content vertically centred, same gutters; still slides over the last work case. Header Get in touch opens the drawer (`data-drawer-open`); every other `#contact` link (hero Book a consultation, nav Contact) smooth-scrolls and focuses the e-mail field. No contact ASCII gem. Option 1, Option 2 and `option-2-cursor-reel/` are not part of this option.
- Further options (Option 4, …) will be added as separate designs. Record each one here with its files/location when it's created.

**Rule: if it's not 100% clear which option a prompt applies to, ask the user before making changes.** Don't assume the most recently edited option, and don't apply a change to several options unless explicitly told to.
